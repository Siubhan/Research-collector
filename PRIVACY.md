# Privacy Policy — Research Collector

**Last updated: 2026-06-27**

## Data collection

Research Collector does not collect, transmit, or share any user data.

All information you save (quotes, source URLs, session names) is stored exclusively in your browser using the `browser.storage.local` API. This data:

- never leaves your device
- is not sent to any server
- is not accessible to the extension's developer or any third party
- is deleted when you uninstall the extension or clear browser storage

## Permissions used

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Save quotes and sessions locally in the browser |
| `activeTab` | Read the URL and title of the current tab when saving a quote |
| `contextMenus` | Add "Save quote" to the right-click menu |
| `downloads` | Export sessions as JSON or TXT files to your device |
| `tabs` | Show a confirmation toast on the page after saving |

No permission is used to read page content beyond what you explicitly select and save.
